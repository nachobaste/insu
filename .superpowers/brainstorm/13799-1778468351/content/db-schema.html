Black-Scholes Mapping for Parametric Insurance

S = Current probability of event (0–1), derived from oracle data + historical frequency
K = 1.0 (normalized strike — event either happens or not)
T = Days until trigger_deadline / 365
σ = Historical volatility of event probability (rolling 90-day std dev of daily frequency)
r = Risk-free rate (SOFR, ~5%)
Model = Binary cash-or-nothing call: Premium = e−rT × N(d₂) × Payout
Fee = Insu takes 5–10% spread on top of fair premium
